RUNBOOK: Power Cap Escalation Response
Trigger: Node power draw exceeds 90% of rated cap for more than 2 minutes
Severity Levels:
  - WARNING: 90-94% — monitor, prepare load shed plan
  - HIGH: 95-98% — execute load shed 25% to peer node
  - CRITICAL: 99%+ — emergency load shed 50%, risk of hardware protection shutdown

Step 1: Confirm power reading is sustained, not a transient spike
Step 2: Check if new model deployment changed power envelope — compare to baseline
Step 3: At WARNING: pre-position peer node to accept overflow traffic
Step 4: At HIGH: shed 25% of lowest priority requests to peer node immediately
Step 5: Verify peer node power headroom before accepting overflow
Step 6: At CRITICAL: shed 50% load, notify customer of degraded capacity
Step 7: Do not attempt to push node past rated cap — hardware protection shutdown causes full outage
Step 8: Update node power profile if new model permanently raises power envelope
Recovery: Return to full load only after sustained reading below 85% for 5 minutes
