RUNBOOK: Cascading Failure Prevention Protocol
Trigger: Two or more nodes showing simultaneous degradation signals
Severity: Always CRITICAL — cascade risk compounds every minute

Step 1: Immediately assess if failures are correlated — shared cooling, shared network, shared power circuit
Step 2: If correlated root cause suspected — do not failover to nodes on same circuit or rack
Step 3: Identify truly independent failover targets — different rack, different power feed, different network path
Step 4: Prioritize stabilizing the least degraded node first — prevent it from becoming the next failure
Step 5: Activate cross-tier emergency protocol if same-tier nodes all compromised
Step 6: Implement request queuing for non-latency-sensitive workloads immediately
Step 7: Notify all affected customers simultaneously — do not stagger notifications during cascade
Step 8: Engage infrastructure and networking teams in parallel — cascade requires multi-team response
Step 9: Establish 5-minute check-in cadence until all nodes stable
Recovery: Cascade cleared only when all nodes hold below 80% utilization for 10 consecutive minutes
