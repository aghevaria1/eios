RUNBOOK: Thermal Escalation Response
Trigger: Node temperature exceeds 80C or rises more than 5C in 10 minutes
Severity Levels:
  - WARNING: 80-84C — monitor, reduce batch size 20%
  - HIGH: 85-88C — shed 40% load to peer node, alert on-call
  - CRITICAL: 89C+ — emergency failover, throttle to minimum, page engineering

Step 1: Identify affected node and current temperature trend (rising or plateauing)
Step 2: Check utilization — if above 85%, reduce batch size immediately
Step 3: If temperature still rising after 2 minutes, initiate load shed to peer node
Step 4: Verify peer node has headroom (util below 80%, temp below 75C)
Step 5: Migrate active sessions gracefully — complete in-flight requests before rerouting
Step 6: If no peer headroom available, activate cross-tier emergency protocol
Step 7: Notify customer SLA owner if breach window exceeds 3 minutes
Step 8: Log incident with full thermal timeline for RCA
Recovery: Return traffic to original node only after 10 minutes of stable temperature below 75C
