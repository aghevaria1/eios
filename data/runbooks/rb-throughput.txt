RUNBOOK: Throughput Degradation Response
Trigger: Tokens/sec drops more than 30% from baseline in under 5 minutes
Severity Levels:
  - WARNING: 30-50% drop — diagnose cause before acting
  - HIGH: 50-75% drop — immediate load shed, parallel diagnosis
  - CRITICAL: 75%+ drop or zero — emergency failover, assume hardware fault

Step 1: Correlate throughput drop with other metrics — util, temp, memory, power
Step 2: If util is LOW but throughput is LOW — suspect NVLink, PCIe, or network fault
Step 3: If util is HIGH and throughput is LOW — suspect memory pressure or thermal throttle
Step 4: If util is NORMAL and throughput is LOW — suspect software bottleneck or driver issue
Step 5: For hardware fault pattern: initiate immediate failover, do not attempt software fix
Step 6: For software bottleneck: restart model serving process on node without full failover
Step 7: For network fault: reroute through secondary switch path
Step 8: Notify customer if throughput SLA breach window exceeds 2 minutes
Recovery: Validate throughput at 95% of baseline for 3 consecutive minutes before clearing incident
